Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

At Chatrix, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our unfiltered emotional companion platform.

1. Data Security & Encryption

Chat transcripts, relationship states, and companion memories are encrypted locally on your device before being transmitted or stored. We use modern secure protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

2. Guest Sessions

For guest sessions, conversation transcripts are temporarily stored in your local browser cache. Once you migrate to an authenticated account, these logs are transferred securely to your cloud profile and cleared from local storage.

3. Children's Privacy

Chatrix is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Changes to Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated timestamp.

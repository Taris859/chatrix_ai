The Story Behind Chatrix

I built Chatrix because I was tired of AI that felt cold, repetitive, and constantly broke character. I wanted companions that could remember conversations, grow with you, and feel genuinely alive.

Every feature—from relationship memories to personality progression—is something I wished existed myself.

Thank you for being part of this journey.
